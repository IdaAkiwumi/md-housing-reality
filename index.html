Understanding the numbers

What does median mean?

The middle value in an ordered set. Half fall below it and half above. It differs from an average because extreme high or low values do not skew the result.

What does gross rent include?

Contract rent plus the estimated monthly cost of tenant-paid utilities (electricity, gas, water, and sewer) and fuels. It reflects actual costs paid by surveyed households during the five-year survey window, not today's asking rents.

What does the 30 percent benchmark mean?

The annual gross income that would make median monthly rent equal exactly 30 percent of pre-tax income. It is a standard public policy benchmark, not a personal guarantee of housing comfort or landlord approval.

Why margins of error?

Census estimates are based on probability samples, not full counts. A $1,500 rent with a $50 margin of error means there is a 90 percent probability that the true median lies between $1,450 and $1,550.

What is the current market rent figure?

It comes from the Zillow Observed Rent Index (ZORI), a monthly smoothed measure of asking rents across available rental properties in each county. It reflects the open market today, whereas Census ACS is a 5-year survey window ending in 2024.

Zillow does not publish figures for jurisdictions with insufficient listing counts. In Maryland, Kent and Somerset counties typically have no ZORI estimate.

What is renter household income?

From ACS table B25119, this is the median annual income of households that rent their primary home, rather than all households. Across Maryland, renter median income is significantly lower than homeowner and overall household medians.

What can historical trends tell us?

Changes across the 2010 to 2014, 2015 to 2019, and 2020 to 2024 survey windows indicate shifts in nominal dollars. They show independent five-year cross-sections rather than tracking individual households over time.

How is renter housing cost burden calculated?

From ACS table B25070, it calculates the percentage of surveyed renter households whose gross rent represents 30 percent or more of household income. Households where cost burden cannot be calculated (such as those reporting zero or negative income, or paying no cash rent) are excluded from the denominator. Small differences between counties are sample variations and not statistically proven rankings.

What does the refresh date mean?

The date this application retrieved the Census and Zillow datasets, not the original release date of the Census Bureau tables.

Technical calculations and data references

Census median rent: table B25064. All-household median income: table B19013. Renter median income: table B25119 (variable 003E). Owner-occupied home value: table B25077. Renter cost burden: table B25070 (categories 007 through 010 divided by total computable renters). Market rent: Zillow ZORI county-level smoothed seasonally adjusted index.

30% income benchmark: monthly rent × 12 ÷ 0.30. Personal rent share: monthly rent × 12 ÷ visitor income × 100.

Privacy

No analytics, cookies, or tracking scripts. Two web fonts load from Google Fonts. Income and calculator entries stay strictly in your local browser tab. If you subscribe to Rent Watch, your email address is handled by Buttondown and never shared.

Why this exists

I am Ida Akiwumi, a UX designer. After being laid off last summer, I started thinking about how I could use my UX and data skills to build something useful beyond my next job.

Housing affordability affects communities across Maryland, but understanding how much rent burdens people can mean digging through multiple Census tables and overlapping survey periods. I wanted to make that information easier to find, compare, and explore in one place.

So I built Maryland Housing Burden, a free tool comparing renter burden across all 24 Maryland jurisdictions, alongside data that helps put reported rents into context.

The data refreshes automatically every month. No ads, no tracking, no cookies.
